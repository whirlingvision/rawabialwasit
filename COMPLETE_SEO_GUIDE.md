## On-Page SEO

### 1. Title Tags (Meta Titles)

**Current Status:** ✅ Implemented on 4 pages, ❌ Need to add to 24 pages

**Best Practices:**
- Length: 50-60 characters
- Include primary keyword at the beginning
- Include brand name "Rawabi Alwasit"
- Unique for each page
- Include location if relevant (Saudi Arabia, Dammam)

**Example Format:**
```
[Primary Keyword] - Rawabi Alwasit Company | [Location/Service Type]
```

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Rawabi Alwasit Company - Industrial Supplies & Services Provider in Saudi Arabia"
- ✅ "Contact Us - Rawabi Alwasit Company | Get Quote & Inquiry"
- Need: "Steel Products in Saudi Arabia - Rawabi Alwasit Company | Industrial Steel Supplier"
- Need: "Safety Equipment Dammam - Rawabi Alwasit Company | PPE Supplier Saudi Arabia"

### 2. Meta Descriptions

**Best Practices:**
- Length: 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword
- Include call-to-action (CTA)
- Unique for each page
- Compelling and descriptive

**Example Format:**
```
[What you offer] in [Location]. [Key benefits]. Contact [Phone/Email] for [Service/Product]. [Location] industrial supplier.
```

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Contact Rawabi Alwasit Company for industrial supplies and services. Get quotes, inquiries, and professional consultation. Located in Dammam, Saudi Arabia."
- Need: "Professional steel products supplier in Dammam, Saudi Arabia. High-quality steel, stainless steel, and aluminium from Rawabi Alwasit. Get quote today!"

### 3. Header Tags (H1, H2, H3)

**Best Practices:**
- One H1 per page (most important keyword)
- H1 should match or closely match title tag
- H2 for main sections
- H3 for subsections
- Include keywords naturally

### 4. Image Alt Tags

**Best Practices:**
- Descriptive and specific
- Include relevant keywords when appropriate
- Don't stuff keywords
- Describe the image accurately
- Use for decorative images too (but can be empty: alt="")

**Current Status:** ⚠️ Need audit - many images may be missing alt tags
